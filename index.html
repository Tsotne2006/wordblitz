How To Play

  1. Guess the 5-letter word.
  2. Green means the letter is correct and in the right spot.
  3. Yellow means the letter is correct but in the wrong spot.
  4. Gray means the letter isn't in the word at all.
  5. If a letter shows up yellow more than once, the word contains that letter multiple times.
  6. Guess as many words as you can in 60 seconds, then submit your run with a name to land on the leaderboard.
